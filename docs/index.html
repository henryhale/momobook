Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how MomoBook handles your messages and your money data.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. MomoBook doesn't request internet access, so it can't send anything anywhere. Your journal is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Does it read my personal messages?

Only transaction messages from supported mobile money and bank senders are saved. Everything else is skipped and never stored.

Which networks and banks are supported?

Mobile money and bank SMS from Uganda (UGX); see the list at the top of this page. More channels and countries are planned. To request one, open an issue with a few sample messages, with personal details removed.

What if I delete an SMS?

Your journal keeps its own copy of each transaction message, so the entry stays even after the SMS is gone.

Is it free?

Yes. MomoBook is free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license, with no ads.

Can I use it without SMS access?

Yes. You can paste the text of a message into the app yourself and it will be added to your journal.
