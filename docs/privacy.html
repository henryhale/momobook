Privacy policy

Your money data stays on your phone.

MoMoBook reads your mobile money and bank messages on your phone to build a spending journal. The app has no internet access, so nothing is ever sent to us or anyone else.

  • No account and no cloud
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • Personal messages are ignored

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the MoMoBook Android app (com.momobook.app), developed by Henry Hale, handles your information.

What the app accesses

  • SMS messages. With your permission (Read SMS and Receive SMS), MoMoBook reads messages in your SMS inbox and new messages as they arrive.
  • Messages you paste. You can paste the text of a mobile money or bank message into the app yourself.

How the information is used

MoMoBook looks for transaction messages from supported channels, currently MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money and Centenary Bank. From those messages it records the amount, fees, balance, date, counterparty and transaction ID in a journal, so you can see your deposits, spending and balances over time. A copy of each of these messages is kept in the journal so your history stays even if you delete the SMS.

All other SMS messages, such as personal conversations, are ignored. They are not stored, recorded or used.

Where your data is stored

Your journal is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. MoMoBook has no account system, no server and no cloud backup run by us. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

Sharing

We do not collect, sell or share your data with anyone. MoMoBook contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

If you use Export journal, the app creates a CSV file and opens Android's share sheet. The file goes only to the app or person you choose, and only when you choose to send it.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke the SMS permissions at any time in Android Settings. You can still add messages by pasting them.
  • You can delete all MoMoBook data at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings → Apps → MoMoBook → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Children

MoMoBook is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
